# Topology, error correction, and generalized symmetry: research map

Verified against primary arXiv abstracts, submission histories, journal records, and selected full texts on 22 October 2026. The scope includes versions posted by that date. This is a research and editorial ledger, not a claim that every problem has a settled classification.

## Conceptual dependency map

Density matrices and distinguishability → stabilizer measurements → syndrome-conditioned probabilities → inference of an error's homology class → recoverability and coherent information → noise-driven transitions.

Ordinary SSB → gauging → topological loop order → strong/weak higher-form symmetry → anomalous loop algebra → mixed-state topological order. An independent branch is SPT string order → dephasing one symmetry sector → strong/weak SPT protection → boundary and strange-correlator diagnostics → anomaly constraints. Non-Abelian symmetry adds irrep labels, multiplicity spaces, and fusion records; a list of local charges is then insufficient for optimal decoding.

## Essential distinctions

- A decoder's threshold is algorithm dependent; an optimal-recovery threshold is an information-theoretic property of the noise model and code family.
- A fixed pure codeword density matrix, the maximally mixed code-space density matrix, and a state entangled with a logical reference answer different questions. Quantum-memory diagnostics require the reference or an equivalent all-logical-state criterion.
- Noise histories with the same syndrome can differ by stabilizers or by logical operators. Maximum likelihood sums histories in an equivalence class; minimum weight picks a representative and need not be optimal.
- The von Neumann/fidelity and Rényi-2 transitions can differ. A transition of a doubled Choi state is not automatically a stable phase boundary under physically motivated local recovery.
- “Intrinsically mixed” is relative to an equivalence relation. Two-way quasilocal channels, locally reversible channel circuits, stochastic local channels, and finite Rényi-2 Markov-length paths preserve different data.
- Finite-size experimental correlators are signatures of the theoretical order and do not establish an infinite-volume singularity by themselves.

## Primary-source ledger

### Error correction, decoding, and statistical mechanics

1. Eric Dennis, Alexei Kitaev, Andrew Landahl, John Preskill, **Topological quantum memory**, J. Math. Phys. 43, 4452–4505 (2002), [quant-ph/0110143](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0110143), DOI 10.1063/1.1499754. Foundational syndrome/homology statistical-mechanics mapping. Distinguish perfect syndrome measurement from repeated faulty measurements.
2. Chenyang Wang, Jim Harrington, John Preskill, **Confinement-Higgs transition in a disordered gauge theory and the accuracy threshold for quantum memory**, Ann. Phys. 303, 31–58 (2003), [quant-ph/0207088](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0207088), DOI 10.1016/S0003-4916(02)00019-2. Noisy syndrome measurements introduce an extra spacetime dimension and a disordered gauge problem.
3. Jong Yeon Lee, Wenjie Ji, Zhen Bi, Matthew P. A. Fisher, **Decoding Measurement-Prepared Quantum Phases and Transitions: from Ising model to gauge theory, and beyond**, [2208.11699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.11699), first 24 Aug 2022, v2 6 Sep 2022. Measurement-prepared states, classical models and decoding without exponentially costly postselection. Useful bridge between retained/discarded classical records.
4. Ruihua Fan, Yimu Bao, Ehud Altman, Ashvin Vishwanath, **Diagnostics of mixed-state topological order and breakdown of quantum memory**, PRX Quantum 5, 020343 (2024), [2301.05689](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05689), DOI 10.1103/PRXQuantum.5.020343. Relative entropy, coherent information and topological negativity are mapped to classical spin observables for the 2D toric code under independent Pauli noise. Coincident optimal threshold is model specific, not a theorem for arbitrary channels.
5. Yimu Bao, Ruihua Fan, Ashvin Vishwanath, Ehud Altman, **Mixed-state topological order and the errorfield double formulation of decoherence-induced transitions**, Phys. Rev. Lett. 136, 220402 (2026), [2301.05687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05687), v2 1 May 2026, DOI 10.1103/6f98-tvb8. Doubled Abelian TQFT with decoherence as a temporal defect and boundary anyon condensation. Applies to the specified Abelian topological setting; not a complete theory of arbitrary non-Abelian noise.

### Mixed SPT, disorder, and dephasing

6. Ruochen Ma, Chong Wang, **Average Symmetry-Protected Topological Phases**, Phys. Rev. X 13, 031016 (2023), [2209.02723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02723). Decorated disorder defects, average anomalies, and important distinction between a disordered ensemble and a density matrix after forgetting its record.
7. Ruochen Ma, Jian-Hao Zhang, Zhen Bi, Meng Cheng, Chong Wang, **Topological Phases with Average Symmetries: the Decohered, the Disordered, and the Intrinsic**, Phys. Rev. X 15, 021062 (2025), [2305.16399](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16399). Unified defect-decoration framework, with different admissibility conditions for disorder and decoherence. Intrinsic average phases exist under stated symmetry and locality definitions.
8. Jong Yeon Lee, Yi-Zhuang You, Cenke Xu, **Symmetry protected topological phases under decoherence**, Quantum 9, 1607 (2025), [2210.16323](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.16323), v6 29 Dec 2024, DOI 10.22331/q-2025-01-23-1607. Type-I/type-II strange diagnostics distinguish accessible information from doubled-space topological response.
9. Ruochen Ma, Alex Turzillo, **Symmetry Protected Topological Phases of Mixed States in the Doubled Space**, PRX Quantum 6, 010348 (2025), [2403.13280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13280). Positivity and Hermiticity constrain Choi-space SPT classifications; not every doubled pure SPT corresponds to a density matrix.
10. Hanyu Xue, Jong Yeon Lee, Yimu Bao, **Tensor network formulation of symmetry protected topological phases in mixed states**, [2403.17069](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17069). Tensor-network classification under its short-range-entanglement/injectivity assumptions; direct-product unitary strong/weak symmetries give the mixed cohomology term in addition to ordinary strong-group SPT invariants.
11. Pablo Sala, Sarang Gopalakrishnan, Masaki Oshikawa, Yizhi You, **Spontaneous strong symmetry breaking in open systems: Purification perspective**, Phys. Rev. B 110, 155150 (2024), [2405.02402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02402). Relates mixed-state order to SPT features of a specified purification. Purification is not unique; statements involving the environment require its preparation structure.
12. Yuchen Guo, Shuo Yang, **Strong-to-weak spontaneous symmetry breaking meets average symmetry-protected topological order**, Phys. Rev. B 111, L201108 (2025), [2410.13734](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13734). Double-ASPT and decorated-domain-wall duality, numerical/analytical phase structure in a specified 1D model.
13. Jeet Shah et al., **Instability of steady-state mixed-state symmetry-protected topological order to strong-to-weak spontaneous symmetry breaking**, Quantum 9, 1912 (2025), [2410.12900](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12900), v2 2 Nov 2025, DOI 10.22331/q-2025-11-17-1912. Reaction-diffusion parent Lindbladian for decohered cluster states; generic allowed strong-defect perturbations destabilize its steady-state SPT, whereas weak-defect-only perturbations can preserve it. This is a distinction between state-phase robustness and dynamical steady-state robustness.
14. Jian-Hao Zhang, Yang Qi, Zhen Bi, **Fidelity Strange Correlators for Average Symmetry-Protected Topological Phases**, Science Bulletin 71, 688–691 (2026), [2210.17485](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17485), DOI 10.1016/j.scib.2025.12.041. Fidelity-based bulk diagnostic for ASPT, long-range/power-law examples. Latest arXiv revision is v3, 2 Feb 2025; journal publication is 2026. Do not infer a 2026 arXiv revision from the review's citation year.
15. Shuangyuan Lu, Lucas Q. Silveira, Yizhi You, **Nonequilibrium topological response under charge dephasing**, [2602.23484](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23484), first 26 Feb 2026. Decorated G defects carrying S charge generate a dephasing-to-SWSSB mechanism; higher-form, modulated, and gapless extensions are model/framework results.
16. Yoshihito Kuno, Takahiro Orito, **Hierarchy of mixed symmetry protected topological states in extended cluster states under subsystem decoherence**, [2606.22507](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22507), first 21 Jun 2026. Subsystem decoherence hierarchy with Rényi-2 string orders. Retain the diagnostic qualifier.
17. Yoshihito Kuno, Takahiro Orito, **Fate of average symmetry-protected topological states under symmetry-preserving quantum operations**, [2609.27530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27530), first 23 Sep 2026. Choi-space stabilizers, MPS filtering and no-click postselection. Non-trace-preserving filtering must not be conflated with an unconditioned CPTP channel.

### Higher-form symmetry, anomalies, and intrinsic mixed order

18. Zijian Wang, Zhengzhi Wu, Zhong Wang, **Intrinsic Mixed-state Topological Order**, PRX Quantum 6, 010314 (2025), [2307.13758](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13758). Fermionic anyon proliferation can remove quantum memory while retaining topological negativity; strong anomalous symmetries constrain preparation.
19. Ramanjit Sohal, Abhinav Prem, **A Noisy Approach to Intrinsically Mixed-State Topological Order**, PRX Quantum 6, 010313 (2025), [2403.13879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13879). “Gauging out” anyons, subsystem-code interpretation, nonmodular anyon theories. Their category proposal is partial, not a completed classification.
20. Tyler D. Ellison, Meng Cheng, **Toward a Classification of Mixed-State Topological Orders in Two Dimensions**, PRX Quantum 6, 010315 (2025), [2405.02390](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02390), v3 21 Jan 2025. Two-way quasilocal-channel framework; incoherent proliferation leaves commutants, classical gauging can add transparent bosons. Premodular classification is explicitly a conjecture.
21. Carolyn Zhang, Yichen Xu, Jian-Hao Zhang, Cenke Xu, Zhen Bi, Zhu-Xi Luo, **Strong-to-weak spontaneous breaking of 1-form symmetry and intrinsically mixed topological order**, Phys. Rev. B 111, 115137 (2025), [2409.17530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17530), v3 19 Feb 2026. Latest preprint uses a finite continuously varying Rényi-2 Markov-length relation; disorder-averaged toric-code phases and perturbative stability. Earlier-paper terminology should not erase revised assumptions.
22. Leonardo A. Lessa, Shengqi Sang, Tsung-Cheng Lu, Timothy H. Hsieh, Chong Wang, **Higher-form anomaly and long-range entanglement of mixed states**, [2503.12792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12792). Stochastic local channels mod out classical correlations. Strong 1-form anomalies imply bipartite LRE in 2+1 dimensions; higher-dimensional multipartite extension is conjectural.
23. Shengqi Sang, Leonardo A. Lessa, Roger S. K. Mong, Tarun Grover, Chong Wang, Timothy H. Hsieh, **Mixed-state phases from local reversibility**, [2507.02292](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02292), v2 25 Jul 2025. Local reversibility preserves weak symmetry data, with weak unitaries dressed into channel symmetries. Classical loop ensemble separates this definition from two-way connectivity.
24. Po-Shen Hsin, Ryohei Kobayashi, Abhinav Prem, **Higher-Form Anomalies Imply Intrinsic Long-Range Entanglement**, [2504.10569](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10569), v2 5 Nov 2025. Higher-form anomaly obstruction to SRE, 3+1D mixed example without remote detectability. State the finite-symmetry/gap assumptions accompanying each bound.
25. Leonardo A. Lessa, Meng Cheng, Chong Wang, **Mixed-State Quantum Anomaly and Multipartite Entanglement**, Phys. Rev. X 15, 011069 (2025), DOI [10.1103/PhysRevX.15.011069](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.15.011069). Cohomological anomalous strong symmetry implies (d+2)-nonseparability: proof for d≤1, plausibility arguments for higher dimensions. Strong/weak mixed anomalies constrain correlations but need not force quantum entanglement.
26. Tai-Hsuan Yang, Bowen Shi, Jong Yeon Lee, **Topological Mixed States: Phases of Matter from Axiomatic Approaches**, Phys. Rev. X 16, 041002 (2026), [2506.04221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04221), v3 5 Oct 2026, DOI 10.1103/3bpt-f9pd. Local recoverability, absence of long-range correlations, spatial uniformity; classical/quantum logical memories and non-Abelian secret-sharing constraints.
27. Zijian Song, Jian-Hao Zhang, **Strong-to-weak spontaneous symmetry breaking of higher-form non-invertible symmetries in Kitaev's quantum double model**, [2509.24179](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24179), v3 20 Sep 2026. Decohered non-Abelian quantum doubles, locally indistinguishable information convex. Latest wording: number of extremal points, not affine dimension, equals pure ground-state degeneracy.

### Holography and fusion

28. Sakura Schafer-Nameki, Apoorv Tiwari, Alison Warman, Carolyn Zhang, **SymTFT Approach for Mixed States with Non-Invertible Symmetries**, [2507.05350](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05350). Doubled-space categorical-symmetry classification framework and lattice examples.
29. Marvin Qi, Ramanjit Sohal, Xie Chen, David T. Stephen, Abhinav Prem, **The Symmetry Taco: Equivalences between Gapped, Gapless, and Mixed-State SPTs**, [2507.05335](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05335), v2 2 Oct 2025. Folded bilayer SymTFT, relation between igSPT and intrinsic average SPT; classification restricted to specified short-range-correlated Choi states.
30. Tsung-Cheng Lu, Yu-Jie Liu, Sarang Gopalakrishnan, Yizhi You, **Holographic duality between bulk topological order and boundary mixed-state order**, PRX Quantum 7, 033041 (2026), [2511.19597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19597), v2 1 Sep 2026. Repeated-channel/isoTNS construction: boundary SWSSB and CMI inherited from higher-dimensional topological structure.
31. Akash Vijay, Jong Yeon Lee, **Holographically Emergent Gauge Theory in Symmetric Quantum Circuits**, [2511.21685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21685), v2 8 May 2026. Symmetric random circuits, bulk noisy surface code, boundary/bulk coherent information and charge sharpening.
32. Pablo Sala, Vlad Temkin, Cenke Xu, Daniel Podolsky, Ehud Altman, **Climbing the Fusion Tree: Center Ordering and Decoding Hierarchy in Non-Abelian Mixed States**, [2610.06692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06692), first 5 Oct 2026. Nested-region fusion records define progressively stronger decoders. Finite-level conclusions for nonnilpotent groups differ from a theorem for an unrestricted optimal decoder. SU(2) numerical center transition and S3 example are valuable worked frontiers.

### Post-review experimental/theory bridge (shared with frontier chapters)

33. Qian Cao, Zhen Bi, Kater W. Murch, **Mixed-State Symmetry-Protected Topology and Strong-to-Weak Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking in a Superconducting Qubit Array**, [2608.24993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24993), 25 Aug 2026. Five-qubit finite-size signatures.
34. Christopher Fechisin et al., **Mixed-state phases induced by power-law quantum channels**, [2610.00480](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00480), first submitted **30 Sep 2026**. Long-range dephasing circumvents the finite-depth local 1D obstruction, with Rényi-2 mixed-SPT and SWSSB phase diagram.
35. Zhi-Yuan Wei et al., **Strong-to-Weak Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking Transitions on a Quantum Processor**, [2610.26229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26229), first submitted **19 Oct 2026**, hence included despite high arXiv sequence number. Two-setting reconstruction uses prior knowledge of the noise channel. Finite-size crossings/scaling are reported, not a literal thermodynamic-limit measurement.

## Textbook derivations to reconstruct

1. Stabilizer projector and exact entropy formula; error classes and posterior distribution.
2. Toric-code chain boundary, homology and posterior partition functions; derive Nishimori matching rather than introduce an unexplained temperature.
3. Reference-entangled code: derive I_c = k ln2 − H(logical error | syndrome) for a Pauli channel with perfect syndrome extraction.
4. Pure-codeword versus code-space examples; explain why classical information can survive after quantum information is lost.
5. Cluster stabilizers, protected edge Pauli algebra, sublattice dephasing projector, explicit remaining strong symmetry and weak other symmetry; derive string attenuation.
6. Classical closed-loop ensemble as a probability distribution: local marginals, noncontractible sector labels, and lost quantum coherence.
7. Anticommuting crossing strings and the origin of the toric-code mutual anomaly; explain what the mixed-state strong/weak conditions actually preserve.
8. SU(2) four-spin singlet multiplicities and ambiguity of local charge records; nested fusion measurements and data-processing monotonicity of optimal decoding success.

## Gaps and claims requiring restraint

A universal mixed-state phase equivalence is not agreed upon. Premodular categories are a partial/proposed classification; higher-dimensional anomaly/entanglement implications have theorem-specific domains. Stable state features, stable stationary phases, and preparation-time obstructions are distinct. Topological negativity is useful but neither a universal substitute for quantum capacity nor a complete invariant. Doubled-space R2 long-range order can occur at different noise from fidelity order. Non-Abelian groups require more than transplanting a one-dimensional charge-sector formula. No additional unknown paper is silently assumed from author names or review “to appear” references.
